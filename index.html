About & references ▾

Chart selection. Fenton 2025 is used while corrected gestational age (CGA) is ≤ 50 weeks; after that the WHO Child Growth Standards (0–5 y) are used by corrected age (CGA − 40 weeks).

Centiles & z-scores are estimated by interpolating between the P3, P15, P50, P85 and P97 curves (and between weeks/months). Values beyond P3/P97 are extrapolated — treat them as approximate.

Data are saved only on this device (no server). Use Patients → Back up to keep a copy or move patients to another phone.

References. Fenton TR et al., third-generation preterm growth chart, 2025 (fentongrowth.ca). WHO Multicentre Growth Reference Study Group, Acta Paediatrica 2006; Suppl 450.
