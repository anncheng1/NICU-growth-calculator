About & references ▾

Chart selection. Fenton 2025 is used up to 50 weeks corrected gestational age (CGA). After that the WHO Child Growth Standards (0–5 y) are used — by corrected age (CGA − 40 weeks) until the child is 2 years old, then by actual (chronological) age. From 2 years WHO uses the standing-height standard.

Age display. Up to 40 weeks CGA and for the first month after term, chronological age is shown as day of life (DOL) with CGA, and corrected age in days from the term-equivalent date (not shown before term). From 1 month after term, only chronological and corrected age are shown, in months and days.

Centiles & z-scores are calculated with the LMS method, using the same P3, P10, P50, P90 and P97 curves as the printed charts. WHO values use the official WHO LMS tables (daily); weight z-scores beyond ±3 use WHO's restricted method.

Fenton 2025 data. The Fenton 2025 LMS tables are not published. The curves in this app were digitised from the official 2025 boys' and girls' charts (fentongrowth.ca) and LMS-smoothed; they match the printed curves to a median of about 7 g for weight and 0.03 cm for length and head circumference. The charts state they equal the WHO standard at 50 weeks, so the last week (49–50 weeks) blends into WHO. Weight and head circumference start at 22⁴⁄₇ weeks and length at 23⁴⁄₇ weeks, as on the printed charts. Z-scores may differ slightly from the official Fenton calculator — check borderline values at fentongrowth.ca.

Data are saved only on this device (no server). Use Patients → Back up to keep a copy or move patients to another phone.

References. Fenton TR, Elmrayed S, Alshaikh BN et al. Fenton third-generation growth charts of preterm infants without abnormal fetal growth. Paediatr Perinat Epidemiol 2025;39:543–555. WHO Multicentre Growth Reference Study Group. WHO Child Growth Standards, 2006.
